### Concepts

Large Cap, Liquid Security means a market capitalization greater than USD 10,000,000,000 and less than 1 day of holdings.

**ADTV** means the median of the previous 22 trading days’ volume.

**“Days of Holdings”** for security i means the absolute value of the net number of security i shares (including share equivalents for security i equity swap positions) held by the Portfolio divided by security i’s ADTV (denominated in shares per day). The incremental portion of any position (both long and short) in any given security that is greater than 3 Days’ of Holdings limit will not be margined.

**“Covered Equity Position”** means (i) Equities and (ii) equity swaps, ETF’s and equity index futures with the same underlying equity. For example, IBM would be the “Covered Equity Position” if the Fund held IBM Equities, an equity swap on IBM, and an ETF with IBM as one of its components.

**Base Margin Requirement** means the greater of (i) sections A; (ii) the largest aggregated Short Issuer Stress loss in the Portfolio, and (iii) the Top 5 requirement.

**Short Issuer Stress** means, with respect to each Covered Equity Position in the Portfolio the loss resulting from a Y% increase in the price of such Covered Equity Position, where Y is determined with the below grid:

| Market Capitalization                    | Stress |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ |
| < USD 100 million                        | 1000   |
| >= USD 100 million and < USD 250 million | 750    |
| >= USD 250 million and < USD 5 billion   | 500    |
| >= USD 5 billion and < USD 10 billion    | 300    |
| >= USD 10 billion and < USD 15 billion   | 200    |
| >= USD 15 billion and < USD 20 billion   | 150    |
| >= USD 20 billion                        | 100    |

**Top 5 Requirement** means the sum of the five largest negative PNLs resulting from independently stressing each issuer up or down 50%.

**Issuer grade** is determined with market capitalization and day of holdings as stated:

| Issuer Grade | Market Capitalization                |      | Day of Holdings   |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---- | ----------------- |
| High Grade   | More than or equal to USD 10 billion | AND  | Less than 0.5 day |
| Medium Grade | More than or equal to USD 3 billion  | AND  | Less than 1 day   |
| Low Grade    | Less than USD 3 billion              | OR   | More than 1 day   |



### Section A: Initial Requirement

The requirement on the Portfolio shall be the sum of

a) 12.5% of the GMV to the subset of the Equity Sub-Portfolio that is less than a GMV of

$1,000,000,000. The incremental portion, if any, of the Equity Sub Portfolio GMV that exceeds a

GMV of $1,000,000,000 will receive an additional requirement of 35%, and

b) 20% of the absolute value of the NMV of the Portfolio if Long/Short ratio is higher than 1.05 or lower than 0.95



### Section A: Additional Issuer Country Specific Requirements

#### Single Country Conditions

An additional 10% requirement will be applied on the absolute value of the NMV of the subset of the Portfolio consisting of issuers organized in Tier 1 country that exceeds 10% of the GMV.

An additional 10% requirement will be applied on the absolute value of the NMV of the subset of the Portfolio consisting of issuers organized in Tier 2 country that exceeds 5% of the GMV.

An additional 15% requirement will be applied on the absolute value of the NMV of the subset of the Portfolio consisting of issuers organized in Tier 3 country that exceeds 5% of the GMV.

An additional 15% requirement will be applied on the absolute value of the NMV of the subset of the Portfolio consisting of issuers organized in Tier 4 country that exceeds 2.5% of the GMV.

#### Aggregate Country Conditions

The GMV attributable to Tier 2 countries will receive a 2.5% requirement

The GMV attributable to Tier 3 countries for Large Cap, Liquid Securities will receive a 10% requirement. Tier 3 Non-Large Cap, Liquid Securities will receive 15%.

The GMV attributable to Tier 4 countries for Large Cap, Liquid Securities will receive a 15% requirement. Tier 4 Non-Large Cap, Liquid Securities will receive 20%.

 

### Section A: Additional Liquidity Specific Requirements

An additional requirement of 15% will be applied on that portion of the absolute value of a position in any

given security that is greater than 0.2 Day of Holdings but less than or equal to 1 Days of Holdings.

An additional requirement of 35% will be applied on that portion of the absolute value of a position in any given security that is greater than 1 Days of Holdings but less than or equal to 2 Days of Holdings.

An additional requirement of 50% will be applied on that portion of the absolute value of a position in any given security that is greater than 2 Days of Holdings but less than or equal to 3 Days of Holdings.



### Section A: Additional Sector Specific Requirements

An additional 15% requirement will be applied on the absolute value of the NMV of the subset of the Portfolio consisting of position in any single GICS Industry Sector that exceeds 15% the GMV



### Portfolio Conditions Sections

 Each of the Portfolio Conditions will be calculated independent of all other Portfolio Conditions unless otherwise stated below and all additional requirements will be aggregated and added to the Base Margin Requirement.

• The absolute value of the Issuer Exposure attributable to any High Grade Issuer shall be less than 2% of the Portfolio GMV. The margin requirement on the incremental portion, if any, of any position with Issuer Exposure that is greater than or equal to 2% of the Portfolio Gross Market Value shall be 100%.

• The absolute value of the Issuer Exposure attributable to any Medium Grade Issuer shall be less than 1.5% of the Portfolio GMV. The margin requirement on the incremental portion, if any, of any position with Issuer Exposure that is greater than or equal to 1.5% of the Portfolio Gross Market Value shall be 100%.

• The absolute value of the Issuer Exposure attributable to any Low Grade Issuer shall be less than 0.8% of the Portfolio GMV. The margin requirement on the incremental portion, if any, of any position with Issuer Exposure that is greater than or equal to 0.8% of the Portfolio Gross Market Value shall be 100%.
